# Viper Sortie: notes for Claude

Mobile-first 3D F-16 combat flight sim. Static site, no framework. See README.md for the file layout. Developer / publisher name: **ValidAirsoft Productions** (title screen credit, README, package author).

## Modules
In Career a stock aircraft flies with penalties (`STOCK` in progress.js: -6 % thrust, -10 % roll, half countermeasures, -10 % missile range and agility, no drop tanks, half the missile load). The hangar **Mods** tab (`modsRender` in progressui.js) lists `MODS`: research with the RP earned in that aircraft (state `'r'` in `prog.mods[id]`), then buy with Silver Lions (`true`). Tier I modules lift the penalties and unlock pylons (`tnk1`, `ord1` gate `ldCap` in ops.js); tier II goes beyond stock. Free play ignores modules. Model size corrections live in `M2K` (roster.js).

## Menu notes
`src/menu.js` builds the AAA-style menu: title screen, a left-hand list (Campaign, Quick mission, Online, Hangar, Training, Settings), and panes for each. The original cycle buttons (bTime, bWx, bMission, bPal ...) live hidden in `#legacy`; the "LABEL ... < Value >" rows, sliders and action rows drive them with `drive(btn, getter, want)` and re-read the real game variables in `uiSync()`. Tests that need a setting click the row (`#qRows .mr[data-name=Time] .rm`, `#sRows .mr[...]`) or `document.getElementById(id).click()` for the legacy button. `tools/launch.js` gives test pages the title and the loading delay switched off and a `p.sortie()` helper (Quick mission, then Launch); set `TITLE=1` / `LOAD=1` to keep them. The menu scene is the hangar behind the overlay (`HG.bg`); `camGoal(pane)` eases the camera per pane. Design tokens are CSS variables at the top of `css/style.css` and on `#ov`.
Fonts (Barlow Condensed and Barlow, SIL OFL) are in `fonts/`; the full single-file build embeds them, the cdn build falls back to the Google Fonts link.
Progression extras: battle presets (`PRESETS` in state.js: difficulty, HUD marker level, damage severity, reward multiplier fed into `careerAward`), `progAward` also returns a `breakdown` of named RP and Silver Lion lines for the itemised debrief, daily tasks (`DAILY` in progress.js), and `__vs.endTest({cause,score,kills,t,alive})` to simulate a finished sortie in tests.

## Ground war
`src/groundwar.js` (slice after combat): `WAR_V` lists the War Thunder ground vehicles (M1A1, Leopard 2A4, Challenger, M60A3, M3 Bradley, Marder, Gepard 1A2, M163 Vulcan vs T-80B, T-72A, T-62, BMP-2, ZSU-23-4M4, ZSU-57-2). Red units are pushed into the `ground` list so your weapons, TGP and wingmen treat them as targets; blue units stay in `WAR.units` only. Vehicle models are built in `warBuild` from merged parts (hull, sides, turret, gun). Hits roll damage modules in `warModules` (tracks, engine, gun barrel, turret drive, crew, ammunition rack, fire; `hpSeen` detects hp lost to your bombs and guns too) and `warFlags` shows them on the HUD marker. `updateWar` runs from the main update, shells use their own pool, and the `GROUNDWAR` mission (`setupGroundWar`, `warCheck`) hooks into `sortieStatus` / `sortieCheck`.

## Aircraft roster
Base jets are in `src/data.js`; `src/roster.js` (slice 3, right after render) defines the rest with `dv(base, id, name, callsign, overrides)`, their lofted models (`M2.<id>`, or `m2Alias` to share a hull) and the PAINT / PODS / PITS rows. Tables owned by later slices (GEAR_SPEC, ENG_SND, JET_HP, RCS, FIGHTERS, BANDITS) are filled with `rosterFill(table, rows)` from `ROSTER_LIKE` (copy a row from a similar jet). New aircraft also need a `NODES` row in `src/progress.js` (nation, rank, BR, `req`); a node with no `req` is a root and must be in `PROG_START`. Station counts in `cm.stations` must match the model's `r9` / `r120` nodes; `node tools/roster-test.js` checks this, flies every jet and spawns every type as a bandit.

## Rules that matter
- **Edit `src/*.js`, never `js/game.js` or `js/game.min.js`** (built files). After editing run `npm run build`; `npm test` fails if the bundles are stale. Commit the built files.
- `src/*.js` are ordered slices of ONE strict-mode IIFE (order is in `tools/build.js`). They share scope, so they are not standalone modules and top-level code can only use things defined in earlier slices (or hoisted functions).
- Pure physics lives in `js/physics.js` (unit-tested in Node). Everything else needs a browser.
- three.js is bundled by `npm run build:three` (esbuild) into `vendor/three.min.js`. Light intensities are scaled by `LK` (pi) because r165+ dropped legacy lights.
- Bump the cache name in `sw.js` when shipped files change.

## Keyboard + mouse
Settings > Controls > Input (`vsKbm`, `KBM` in state.js): the mouse is a relative virtual stick (click captures the pointer, Esc releases and pauses), LMB gun, RMB AIM-9, MMB AIM-120, wheel / W,S throttle, A/D rudder. Mouse aim (`KBM.aim`, default): the cursor is a world direction (`KBM.az/el/ad`) and `updatePlayer` rolls and pulls the nose onto it; the other option deflects the stick directly. `tools/kbm-test.js` covers it.

## Key bindings
`KB_ACTS` / `KB_DEF` / `KB` in state.js: every action has up to two keys per control style (`kb` classic, `kbm` mouse mode) plus mouse-button, wheel, sensitivity and invert settings, stored as `vsKeys`. `kbHeld(action)` reads held keys in `updatePlayer`; `actDown` / `actUp` run press actions from the keyboard and the mouse buttons. The editor is `kbOpen` in menu.js (Settings > Controls > Key bindings; `KBE.open` makes the other key handlers stand down). `tools/binds-test.js` covers it.

## Scoring and damage
`scEvent` / `scKill` (state.js) feed the War Thunder style score lines (`SC.list`, drawn in hud.js): hits coalesce, kills chain into double/triple kill bonuses, assists. Player damage is modular, with no hit-point pool: `playerGunHit` / `playerMissileHit` / `wingHit` / `damageSystem` roll the pilot, wings and systems; the jet is lost to a killed pilot, a destroyed airframe (missile), both wings gone or a fuel fire (burning engine + leak). `P.hp` is only derived from the modules in `updateSystems` (smoke, caution lights, eject button, the Arcade HULL bar). AI fighters use the same idea in `aiGunHit` / `aiSystems` (wings, engine fire, controls, pilot; missiles usually kill outright; test drones keep a simple hit-point pool). Systems: engine fire, hydraulics, radar, fuel leak, wings (`P.sys.wL/wR`: lower G limit, roll drift, smoke, can tear off under load), elevator, jammed guns, wounded pilot. `tools/score-test.js` and `tools/damage-test.js` cover them. Tactical map: `TMAP` / `drawMap` in hud.js (Tab or the MAP button in the radar panel; scales 20/50/120 km). Ammunition: `BELTS` / `FUSES` in ops.js (hangar loadout buttons `bBelt`, `bFuse`; gun belt scales air and ground damage, bomb fuse scales blast on hard and soft targets).

## Air battle
`src/battle.js` (slice after groundwar): the `BATTLE` mission (Air domination). `BT` holds points, three capture zones (`BT_ZONES`, radius `BT_R`), the lineup and respawn state; `btTick` (from the main loop, runs while dead too) captures zones, drains points, spawns replacements and ends the battle through `endMission`. AI jets get `e.wp` (the zone to fly to) which `updateAI` follows when they have no target. `die()` calls `btDied` (the sortie continues, `deadT` is held open), the `#btPick` panel lists the lineup, `btRespawn` swaps `AC` via `applyAC` + `resetPlayer`, and `updateCamera` spectates a team-mate meanwhile. The tactical map draws the zones. `tools/battle-test.js` covers it.

## Paint, airbase, effects (Nov 2026)
Paint shader (`paintCompile` in render.js): besides the frame-station panel lines it draws hashed access hatches in some bays, rivet rows beside the frame lines (only up close, faded by `fwidth`), dark walkway outlines on the wing roots, grime streaks running aft, sun-faded upper surfaces and per-panel roughness variation (`roughnessmap_fragment` hook). GLSL statements need a `;` before a closing `}`. Airbase (`buildBase` area in flight.js): arched hangars with doors, six hardened aircraft shelters on a dispersal strip, taxiway links, a tower with a glass cab and roof, a radar dome, fuel tanks, approach light bars and PAPIs. Effects (effects.js): `vaporCone` puts a translucent shader bell (`VC_GEO`, `VC_MAT`, one clone per jet in `obj.userData.vc`) over the wings between Mach 0.92 and 1.05 below 6.5 km; wingtip vortices above 4.2 g; `mslSmoke` lays two puffs a frame for a continuous missile trail; `ventFX` streams fuel mist from a leaking tank or a damaged wing.

## Tanks, sound, cockpit (Nov 2026)
Ground-war vehicles (`warBuild`): a track loop (ground and return runs, sloped ends) with visible road wheels, tyres, hubs, return rollers, sprocket and idler; headlights, tow hooks and exhaust grilles; external fuel drums on red MBTs; turret stowage bins on blue MBTs. Sound (flight.js `sndNear`): two looping voices follow the nearest fighter (2.5 km) and the nearest missile in flight (1.6 km) with distance fall-off, Doppler pitch and stereo pan (`nvPan`); `sndAt(pos, fn)` places one-shots (flyby whoosh, explosions via `sndBoom(v, out)`). Cockpit (cockpit.js): rounded glare-shield lip, console knobs and toggles, and a windscreen frame bow (`fbow`, declared with `let` before `buildOutside` runs) on jets whose canopy has one.

## Tank battle (Nov 2026)
`TANK` mission, `src/tank.js` (slice after battle; the state object is `TNK` because `TK` is the tanker). Free play only for now. The player's tank is a WAR unit with `u.player`: `updateWar` calls `tkDrive` for it instead of the AI, red units target it, and shells, `warModules`, fires and ammo racks work on it as on the AI (`hit.lastBy` and `tkKill` credit kills). `setupTank` finds a dry battlefield, spawns you (`tkType`, Quick mission > Tank row, `vsTank`), four blue allies and seven red vehicles, and point A between them (`tkCheck`: hold it alone for `TK_HOLD` 60 s, or kill all red; losing your tank or red holding A loses). The frame loop skips `updatePlayer` and uses `tkCamera` (third person, or the gunner sight with Z / ZOOM at 14 degrees); `tkRange` finds what is under the reticle and the gun is laid on that point with ballistic drop. `tkHUD` replaces the flight HUD; `#tkUI` is a separate touch overlay (left half drive stick, right half aim) whose buttons use the jet HUD's chamfered `.btn` style with live `<small>` values kept current by `tkButtons` (FIRE lit when loaded, ZOOM, RANGE, AMMO bottom right; VIEW, REPAIR, EXT, pause in a top bar) and `#ctrls` stays hidden. Keys: W A S D drive, arrows aim, Space fire, Z or Shift zoom (the keydown handler hands them to `tkKey`). `tkSound` replaces the jet engine. `warClear` calls `tkClear`. Three vehicles per battle from a lineup: after 3 s of wreck view `tkPicker` lists the blue vehicles not yet used (`TNK.used`; buttons or keys 1-6, auto-pick at 18 s) and `tkRespawn` spawns it at `TNK.start`. Red attack jets (`tkAir`, from the frame loop): an Su-25 pair (`tmpl('red','SU25')`, plain objects in `TNK.air`, kind 'cas') every 65-85 s flies in at 700 m, dives on a blue unit (half the time you), drops a bomb (`TNK.bombs`: 7 damage inside 12 m, less out to 30 m), pulls off and comes back for a second pass; blue AA vehicles fire at them, your shells kill them (`tkAirDown`), and `sndNear` hears them; `warBounce` (groundwar.js) lets an MBT's front arc shrug off some hits, more at range (applies to the AI too); a red arc shows where the last hit came from (`TNK.hitFrom`); a heading-up minimap (2.5 km) sits bottom left. Zoom levels `TNK.zl` 0 third person / 1 ×4 / 2 ×8 (`tkZoom`: Z or ZOOM cycles, + and - step); the laser rangefinder (`tkLase`, R or RANGE) sets `TNK.lased` and the gun is laid on that range (default 800 m), not continuously. Start lines are ~850 m either side of point A, which is a town (`tkTown`: ~30 houses on a street grid round a square and a church with a tower, merged into three meshes); its boxes in `TNK.bld` stop shells (`tkSegHit` in the shell loop), block AI line of sight and push tanks out (`tkPush`). Your own roof ID panel (`mk`) is hidden. FIRE ranges what's under the crosshair first and holds the shot (`TNK.fireQ`, up to 1.5 s) until the turret and gun are laid; shots from a stopped tank scatter 0.4x; zoomed, an enemy within ~1 degree of the crosshair pulls the aim onto it (`TNK.assist`, TRACKING). The crosshair is a plain HUD-coloured cross with a centre dot, no outline (`tkReticle`; the owner disliked a black outline). WT-style extras: `tkResult` shows what your shot did (PENETRATION / NON-PENETRATION / RICOCHET / DESTROYED, plus the modules `warNote` reports for that target) with a hit marker and `sndHit`; VIEW / V toggles the commander's cupola view (`TNK.view`); AMMO / Q switches AP and HE (`s.ammo`: HE flies at 0.62x, does 1.5x to light vehicles and 0.4x to MBTs, splashes 8 m, never ricochets; counts `TNK.apN`/`heN` per vehicle); REPAIR / F fixes tracks, engine, gun and turret drive in 10 s plus 4 s per lost crewman while stopped (`TNK.repT`); EXT / X puts a fire out (two per tank); a module panel (TRK, ENG, GUN, TUR, CREW) shows green, amber or red.; an amber x shows where the gun points only while the turret is still catching up. Canvas gotcha: `rect()` then `arc()` without a `moveTo` joins them with a hairline (that was the line across the gunner sight).

## Enemy pilots (Nov 2026)
`spawnFighter` gives every red fighter (not in Training, not with `opts.noPilot`) a pilot from `pilotFor` (state.js): a tier from `TIER_ORDER` around `difficulty` (18 % one lower, 18 % one higher), a rank and name by nation (`PILOT_NAMES`, nation from `NODES`), and a 5 % + 3 %/wave chance of an ace (tier ACE, callsign from `ACE_CALLS`, 5-18 kills). `e.sk` and `e.gLim` follow the tier. Aces are announced on the radio, tagged in gold on the HUD marker (`★ CALL · kills`, even on Realistic markers inside 12 km) and pay +300 (`ACE KILLED`). Pairs (`spawnPair` sets `mate`): when the target's nose is on one of them (within ~20°, 2.5-14 km) it drags cold as bait for 7-11 s (`mode BAIT`, 28 s cooldown, only tiers with notchP >= .3) while the partner presses; the wingman of a pair brackets 40° off the line of sight between 5 and 20 km (`mode FLANK`).

## Campaign extras (Nov 2026)
ops.js: each enemy sector has a resident ace (`campAce(f)`, stored in `camp.aces`, keeps his tally) who flies in 75 % of CAP and 45 % of other sorties there (`setupCampaign`); shooting him down (`campAceDown`, set in `killEnemy`) takes 20 more off the sector, and if he was within 6 km when you died (`campAceKilledYou`, set in `die`) his tally grows. After each sortie `campaignAfter` may post intel (`camp.intel`, 45 %: that sortie type counts double next time) or a counter-attack (`camp.threat`, 22 % once the front is past sector 3: the next CRUISE sortie must be won or the front falls back a sector). Capturing a sector pays 6,000 Silver Lions. `campBrief()` heads the campaign text; the pane rows are Resistance, Sortie, Sector ace, Record (it must still fit 844x390). `__vs.endTest` now runs `campaignAfter` too.

## Mid-mission surprises (Nov 2026)

Kinds: AMBUSH (a pair 7.5 km behind and low; +200 if both die within 150 s), POPSAM (a SAM 8-12 km ahead on dry ground; +250 within 180 s), MAYDAY (a blue TRANSPORT 'Hog 3' chased by an `escortPref` pair; +300 if the pair dies while it lives, it leaves after 200 s; not in ESCORT), HVT (a red BOMBER as an AWACS 22-28 km ahead running away with an escort pair; +500, escapes after 220 s), WEATHER (rain or overcast via `applyEnv`; `BT.saved` brings your settings back next sortie). ## Hit feedback (Nov 2026)
flight.js: `hitMark(pos, crit)` (called from `hitAI` for the player's hits) draws a white X, or a larger red one for a critical, on the target for a quarter second with a tick sound; `dmgFrom(src)` (bullet and missile hits on you, combat.js) draws a red arc on a circle round the screen centre pointing at the source (camera-relative, top = ahead) for 1.4 s; `startGunCam(e)` (from `killEnemy` for player gun kills on fighters) runs the kill cam in slow motion on the wreck from your side for 1.3 s. `spawnWreck` also flings 4-9 skin fragments (`DEB_MAT`, parts with `small`, gone after 7 s, no ground explosion).

## Combat balance (Nov 2026)
AI radar shots are taken inside 72 % of the launch zone's Rmax (`launchAI` in ai.js), so bandits press in rather than lobbing from the edge. AI flares come in spaced bursts (inside 3 km, every 0.7-1.1 s), and `aiDecoyRoll` scales the per-flare decoy chance by 0.75, or 0.4 for a tail chase. Gun hits on AI fighters register inside 8.5 m and `aiGunHit` is deadlier (pilot 4.5 %, wing +0.2 per hit, engine fires fatal 55 %). Firing the last radar missile reminds the player that landing and stopping at base rearms.

## Guns, flaps, stall
Player gun rounds start at the off-centre barrel and are harmonised on `AC.gunConv` (default 650 m); the pattern opens up with the burst (`gunHeat`, `AC.gunDisp`), one round in four is a visible tracer, and the tracer colour follows the belt (`TRACER_COL`). `fly()` in flight.js handles the player's flaps (`P.flaps` 0/1/2, gear forces landing flaps, `FLAP_CL` / `FLAP_CD` / `FLAP_VMAX`) and stall and spin: jets not matching `SPIN_SAFE` depart below stall speed with the nose high and roll or rudder held (`P.spin`), and recover once the controls are centred and speed returns. In a spin the attitude is set directly (nose down, turning about its own vertical axis; `j.spinYaw`) instead of following the velocity, and the chase camera holds its heading (`P.camYaw`). Touch flaps button `bFlaps` (UP / CBT / LDG) and the `flaps` key action both call `cycleFlaps`. `tools/stall-test.js` covers it.

## Late additions (battle weather, cockpit lights, online zones)

## Performance notes
`mergeStatic` (render.js, called from `buildJet`) merges unnamed static meshes that share a material into one mesh per group, cutting a jet from about 70 draw calls to about 25; named parts (`cs`, `gear`, `canopyG`, `nozG`, `flame`, `wingM`, decals) are never merged. Far ground-war vehicles are hidden beyond 7 / 12 / 20 km (Low / Medium / High). Bloom is off on Medium by default. `__vs.rinfoReset()` / `__vs.rinfo()` read draw calls and triangles (see the perf snippets in the transcript: reset, wait N frames, divide).

## Model upgrades (Nov 2026, round 2)
Round nozzles are petal nozzles (48-segment converging shell with 16 scallops at the exit, lip, dark liner, two flame-holder rings and a tail cone); the afterburner flame group carries four shock diamonds per nozzle (`SHOCK_GEO`/`SHOCK_MAT`). Ground-war running gear (Nov 2026 rebuild): the hull sits between the tracks with sponsons and fenders over them; each track is one extruded loop round the sprocket and idler (`wTrack`) drawn with link stripes from object position (`warTrackMat`; `wMerge` drops UVs, so texture maps don't work on merged war meshes); painted road wheels with rubber tyres, a toothed sprocket, camo side skirts with joints on the box-turret tanks, rubber skirts on Soviet MBTs; Soviet turrets are lathed cast domes (M60 longer); BMP-2 a low cone; MBTs get a gun mantlet, a commander's machine gun and (box turrets) smoke-grenade launchers. Town houses (`tkWallMat`) draw framed windows per floor and roof tile courses in the shader from world position, and some get chimneys; walls also get plaster noise and streaks under the windows, roofs vary tile by tile. Ground-war armour uses `warSkin` (MeshStandard with object-space NATO three-tone or Soviet blotch camouflage from the noise texture, dust and mud low down, grime streaks, roughness variation; wrecks clone it into a plain dark material) and `warMetal` for running gear and fittings. Jet paint feeds `gBump` (panel seams, hatch edges, rivets) into a `normal_fragment_maps` hook so the seams are lit as grooves.

## Ground detail
Terrain shader (render.js): `closeGround` layers smooth mipmapped noise (0.9 / 2.7 / 8 m) for grass variation, dry and green patches and bare earth inside ~700 m, and sand ripples on the desert map (no hashed `floor()` cells: they looked like blocky speckle). `fields()` puts farmland on a noise-warped grid (wavy edges), in muted tones blended 35 % with the base, with faint crop rows that fade by `fwidth` and broken tree lines. A gentle `normal_fragment_maps` bump (strength .22) inside ~900 m (not on snow). The sky shader and the final post pass add a small dither so the sky gradient does not band. Terrain noise is read through `nz()` (two rotated, rescaled samples) so the 256 px noise tile never visibly repeats; `n0` (~11 km) drifts the regional hue; forests have crisp ragged edges (`fo`) and a mottled canopy. Trees (`placeTrees`) are two instanced species, tiered conifers `treesC` and lumpy broadleaf crowns `treesD` (vertex-coloured, `treeMerge`), planted in clumps. Fog is radial and per pixel (render.js patches `THREE.ShaderChunk` fog chunks to use `length(vFogP)`), and the sky below the horizon is the fog colour, so the square edge of the terrain and sea planes never shows. Water waves are band-limited by their own `fwidth` (`WV` macro) and the glint softens with distance. Jet paint: frame seams run in broken lengths (not full rings), hatches are fewer and fainter; nav lights are 7.5 cm bulbs. Every loft jet also gets (end of `buildLoftJet`): NO STEP stencils on the wings, a red ejection-seat triangle and an orange RESCUE arrow under the canopy (decal kinds `nostep`, `eject`, `rescue` in `decalTex`), pale formation-light strips on the fuselage sides, static dischargers on the wingtips and fin tips, AOA vanes behind the radome, and a glare shield plus HUD body under the glass (in the `pilot` group, so hidden in the cockpit view). `LOFT_N` is 36 and `AF_X` has 10 stations (round leading edges). `loftGeo` splines the rows (`loftSmooth`, Catmull-Rom per column, sizes clamped, a row every ~0.45 m; `{smooth:false}` turns it off), so `secAt` (linear) can differ slightly from the drawn surface. Reshape pass (Nov 2026): F-16 tail booms beside the nozzle, wider three-station LERX and a taller canopy; F-15 raked wingtips; Super Hornet LEX out to a wing root on the intake side; curved LERX on MiG-29 and Su-27 families; swept canards on Gripen, Rafale, J-10 and Viggen; flat blended bodies (an `x:0` part, exponent ~4) on F-35, F-22, Su-57, the F-14 pancake with beaver tail, and the MiG-25 deck. `node tools/review-sheet.js OUTDIR IDS` renders six views per jet for this kind of review. Baked AO (render.js `bakeAO(root, key, {res,k})`): rasterises the model into small depth buffers from 40 directions and stores per-vertex `occ` (0 open .. 1 enclosed), testing both hemispheres because generated normals may point inward; `aoPatch` makes materials scale indirect light (and some direct) by it, chaining any existing `onBeforeCompile` and adding '|ao' to `customProgramCacheKey`; meshes without `occ` read 0, so shared materials are safe. Cached per key in `AO_CACHE` ('jet:'+type, 'war:'+name), ~100-150 ms per type; `?noao` turns it off. Menu, hangar and mission setup (`AO_SYNC`, set by `startGame`) bake synchronously; during play `aoNeed` queues a first-time type on a spare copy (`AO_Q`, generator `aoJob`, pumped ~4 ms a frame by `aoPump` from the frame loop) and that first copy has no AO; `aoWarm` (state.js) queues the bandit pool at sortie start. `tools/ui-test.js` is flaky in the sandbox: Chromium sometimes drops file:// localStorage across the reload under load (the reloaded page sees no keys at all); not a game bug. `buildJet` makes paint matte (roughness >= .6, metalness <= .08, envMapIntensity .62). Ground-war hulls: a full-width upper hull whose glacis spans the sponsons plus a narrower lower hull and nose between the tracks; turrets of the box MBTs, Bradley, Marder, Gepard and ZSU-23-4 are faceted slabs (`wSlab` from `WAR_TUR` half outlines via `wHalf`; faces are turned outward automatically), with cupola, hatches, gunner's sight, mantlet, whip antennae, Abrams bustle rack and Challenger TOGS box.

## App updates
Every Android build is signed with the committed `assets/viper-sortie-debug.keystore` (SHA-256 11:EF:CA:AA:...:80:3A), named explicitly in `android/app/build.gradle` by `tools/android-patch.sh` (copying it to `~/.android/debug.keystore` alone did NOT work on the runner: v73 and v74 still got random keys). Check a release with `keytool -printcert -jarfile viper-sortie.apk`. versionCode is the run number. Builds up to v74 used random keys, so going from those to v75 needs one last uninstall. `make-www.js` writes `www/version.json` from `VS_VERSION_NAME`; in the Capacitor app the end of main.js compares it with the latest GitHub release and shows an "Update vNN is available · Download" banner (`#vsUpdate`) linking the release APK. The keystore is a debug key in a public repo: anyone could sign an APK that updates over this one; for Play or wider distribution use the ANDROID_* release secrets instead.

## Windows exe
The `exe` job in `android.yml` (runs after the `apk` job, so the same dispatch and version number publish both) packs `www/` (from `tools/make-www.js`, plus `fonts/`) with Electron (`desktop/main.js`, electron-builder portable) into a single `viper-sortie.exe` and attaches it to the same release. F11 / Alt+Enter toggles full screen; Shift+Esc leaves it. Unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen shows a warning on first run. The sandbox cannot build it; only Actions can.

## Valley strike
The terrain mesh is warped (`buildTerrainGeo`: u -> 0.25u + 0.75u^3) so vertices are about 60 m apart under the player and spread towards the horizon; that is what lets narrow features such as the canyon show.
`VALLEY` mission (ops.js `setupValley` / `valleyCheck` / `valleyStatus`): `valleyRoute` draws a winding centre line `cx(z)` north for about 25 km and the mission sets `CANYON`, which makes `groundH` (render.js `canyonH` / `canProf`) carve a snowy mountain canyon into the terrain: the mission switches to the ALPINE map (your map comes back in `valleyClear`) and lowers the shader snow line (`TERR_U.snowY`, normally 1150) to 120 m, with a 300 m floor at 300 m, rock walls up to a 1,650 m rim (or the natural ground if higher), a bowl round the target, and a mouth that deepens over 2.6 km; `valleyClear` resets it and forces a terrain rebuild (`tCx=NaN`). Gate rings (`valRings`) sit on the centre line (`valRings`, cleared by `clearGround`); two objective bunkers in a bowl with AAA, three SAMs with `g.dormant` (updateGround skips them) and an early-warning radar on the high ground. Above `VAL_CEIL` (150 m AGL) in the radar's line of sight a detection meter fills; full detection or missing the time on target (`VAL_TOT`) calls `valleyAlert` (SAMs wake, a pair scrambles from the north); destroying both bunkers does too, and reaching 9 km from base wins. `effAC` gives jets without ground stores their strike loadout for this mission, as for STRIKE.

## Models
Wings and fins use a NACA 4-digit airfoil section (`wingGeo` in render.js, `AF_X` stations); fuselages loft at `LOFT_N`=28 sides. A deepen pass at the end of roster.js scales every `M2` fuselage (`KF`) and intake/nacelle (`KP`) cross-section, then moves canopies, fins, belly rails and IRST to the new surface and re-runs `m2Fit`. The canopy is a lofted teardrop bubble seated on the fuselage top (`prof(u)` in `buildLoftJet`: raked windscreen, highest over the pilot, tapering aft; height from the `canopy` row, clamped 0.5-0.85 m) with a `spine` fairing behind it. Glass is gold-tinted on F-16/F-22/F-35/F-15EX/F-2, smoke elsewhere. Intake mouths are a lip curling into a black duct; a nose intake (a `mouth` part at x 0 reaching the nose) clips the fuselage just inside the mouth so the inlet is open. MiG-21 and Lightning have a lip row plus a shock cone in the radome colour. Ground targets (`buildSAM`, `buildAAA`, `buildRadar`, `buildBunker`, `buildTruck`, `buildShip` in ops.js) share the `GM` materials and `gBox` / `gCyl` / `gArch` / `gTracks` helpers. Wing planforms were checked against published leading-edge sweep, span and dihedral (Nov 2026: MiG-21 57°, Mirage III/Kfir 60°, Mirage 2000 58°, F1 47°, F-5 32° outer panel, Lightning 60°, Draken 80/57 double delta, anhedral on F-104/F-8/MiG-19/Alpha Jet); model scale corrections in `M2K` still apply on top, so check the dims-test numbers; when you change a wing, move its `r9`/`r120` rails onto the new chord. Loft rows take an optional 7th value, the lower-half exponent (chined noses on the F-22/F-35); intake parts take `rake:[vertical, plan]` for slanted caret mouths; `nozRect` gives 2-D nozzles (F-22). Missiles: `mslGeoFor(name)` in render.js builds one merged, vertex-coloured geometry per missile (lathed body with IR dome or radome, warhead and motor bands; fins by `mslKind`: ir, tail, r73, amr, sarh, big, ram, grid) at the real size from `MSL_DIM`; rails use it through `mslMesh`, and the flying pools in effects.js take it at launch with `mslSet(m, name)`. Radomes use the body grey (`PS.rad` multiplier, default 0.84), not the dark trim colour. Wing-body fillets: `buildLoftJet` adds a concave strip along the main wing's root chord (upper and a smaller lower one) from the wing skin to the fuselage or intake trunk beside it (`sideX` finds that surface; no fillet where the gap is over 0.55 m). Landing gear (`buildGear` in flight.js): lathed tyres on hubs with brake discs, main wheels on a stub axle beside the strut, nose fork or twin-wheel axle, tapered strut, gland nut, chrome oleo, torque-link scissor, drag brace and retraction jack. `node tools/model-sheet.js OUTDIR F16,SU27,g:buildSAM` renders perspective, side and top views of any jet or ground builder for review.
